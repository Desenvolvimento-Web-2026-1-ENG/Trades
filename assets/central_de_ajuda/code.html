Frequently Asked Questions

How do I verify the authenticity of a digital asset? expand_more
All assets on TRADES are authenticated via our proprietary Neon Oracle protocol. You can verify any asset by clicking the "Audit Chain" button on the item detail page, which provides a full provenance history.
What is the 'Elite Trader' status? expand_more
Elite Trader is a tier awarded to accounts with high trading volume and a 100% positive reputation score. Benefits include reduced marketplace fees and early access to "Genesis" drop events.
Can I use hardware wallets with TRADES? expand_more
Yes, TRADES supports all major hardware wallets. For maximum security, we recommend keeping your high-value rare assets in cold storage and only linking them for verification when listing.
